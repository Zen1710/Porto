## What it is

A practice project that shows a catalog of smartphones inside a desktop-style app window. The phone data is sample data written into the code.

## Features

- Search by name, brand, storage, or color
- Filter by brand from the sidebar and sort by name or price
- Open a phone to see its screen, RAM, storage, color, and price
- Pick up to three phones and compare them side by side
- Works with the keyboard and on mobile

## How it is built

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no build step and no dependencies. The phone thumbnails are drawn in code, so the project uses no product photos.
