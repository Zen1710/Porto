## What it is

A one-page portfolio for recruiters, with a detail page for each project and a series of build logs. Adding a project or a log is a matter of adding one MDX file.

## How it works

- Pages are rendered ahead of time with Next.js, so the site is fast and easy to crawl.
- Every entry has a `draft` flag. Drafts show up while I develop locally and never on the live site, and sections with nothing published hide themselves.
- The contact form posts to a Server Action, which validates input with Zod, filters bots with a honeypot and a time trap, and sends the message through Resend.
